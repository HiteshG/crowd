# THE HOUSE ON KAVERI LANE

## Episode 1: The Spill

[SFX: A rhythmic, frantic scrubbing sound. A harsh bristled brush against wet kitchen tiles. Heavy, ragged breathing.]

**MEENAKSHI**
(Panting) 
More bleach, Tara. Hand me the bottle. Quickly.

**TARA**
(Sobbing) 
Ma... Ma, there’s so much blood. It’s everywhere.

[SFX: A plastic bottle is knocked over. Liquid glugs onto the floor.]

**MEENAKSHI**
Watch what you're doing! Look at me. Rohan tripped. He fell against the corner of the granite counter. That is what happened. 

**TARA**
But he's dead! 

**MEENAKSHI**
Swallow it down. Go to the shed. Bring the blue heavy-duty tarp. 

[SFX: Footsteps running. A heavy wooden door opens to a roaring rainstorm. Meenakshi searches Rohan's pockets.]

**MEENAKSHI**
(Muttering) 
Phone... where is your phone, Rohan? 

[SFX: Tara returns, dropping the heavy tarp.]

**MEENAKSHI**
Help me roll him. On three. One. Two. Three.

[SFX: A heavy, wet thud. Grunting with exertion as they drag the tarp.]

**MEENAKSHI**
We carry him to the old compost pit. Keep the flashlight off. We use the lightning.

[SFX: Rain pours. Mud squelches. The sound of rhythmic shoveling, then a heavy thud as the body drops into the earth. The door clicks shut as they return inside.]

**TARA**
(Whispering) 
It's done. Ma... what if someone heard us?

**MEENAKSHI**
The thunder covered the noise. We are safe. 

[SFX: Three loud, sharp knocks on the front door. Extremely aggressive.]

**MALINI**
(Muffled from outside) 
Meenakshi? Are you awake? It's Malini! Open the door!

## Episode 2: The Scent of Bleach

[SFX: The knocking continues, louder. Boom. Boom.]

**TARA**
(Panicked whisper) 
Ma! It's Inspector Malini! She knows! 

**MEENAKSHI**
She knows nothing. Go to the bathroom. Turn on the shower. If she asks, you were bathing. Go!

[SFX: Tara runs. Meenakshi unlocks the heavy door bolts. The door creaks open to the rain.]

**MEENAKSHI**
(Feigning sleepiness) 
Malini? My god, it's almost midnight. Has something happened?

**MALINI**
Sorry to wake you. I'm looking for Rohan. Have you seen him?

**MEENAKSHI**
Rohan? No. Tara and I were watching television until the storm knocked the power out around nine. 

[SFX: Malini steps inside. Heavy police boots on the floor.]

**MALINI**
His bike is missing. Said he had an errand on Kaveri Lane. 

[SFX: Malini walks slowly into the living room, sniffing the air.]

**MALINI**
You clean late, Meenakshi. The house smells like bleach. Very strong. 

**MEENAKSHI**
(Steadily)
The kitchen pipes backed up with the rain. I had to scrub the sink. 

**MALINI**
Mm-hmm. Well, if Tara mentions anything... call me. His mother is worried sick. 

[SFX: Malini exits. The door shuts and bolts slide back into place. Meenakshi exhales a massive, shaky breath.]

**TARA**
(Whispering from the hallway) 
Is she gone?

**MEENAKSHI**
Yes. But tomorrow, we behave exactly as we normally do. You go to school. I teach my classes. 

[SFX: A muffled buzzing sound begins to vibrate from beneath the living room sofa.]

**TARA**
Ma... what is that?

**MEENAKSHI**
(Dread creeping in)
Rohan's phone. 

## Episode 3: The Buried Ring

[SFX: The muffled buzzing continues, vibrating against the wooden floorboards.]

**MEENAKSHI**
Lift the sofa cushions. Now!

[SFX: Cushions being thrown aside. Meenakshi reaches down and pulls out a cracked smartphone. The screen glows.]

**TARA**
Who is it? Is it Malini?

**MEENAKSHI**
It’s his mother. I have to destroy it. If they track the GPS to our house...

[SFX: Meenakshi runs to the kitchen counter. A heavy meat mallet strikes the phone screen. Glass shatters.]

**MEENAKSHI**
(Breathing heavily)
Get my handbag. Tomorrow morning, I will drop the pieces in the river on my way to work. 

**TARA**
Ma, I can't do this. I can't go to school tomorrow and look at his empty desk. I'll break.

**MEENAKSHI**
Listen to me. You are a victim of nothing. Rohan was blackmailing you for the school funds. He was going to ruin us. You defended yourself.

**TARA**
(Crying)
I pushed him!

**MEENAKSHI**
You survived him! Now survive this. 

[SFX: Scene transition. Morning birds chirping. A busy school hallway. A school bell rings.]

**HEADMASTER DAS**
Meenakshi, a word before your first period?

**MEENAKSHI**
Of course, Headmaster.

**HEADMASTER DAS**
Inspector Malini was just here. She’s asking for the attendance logs. And she specifically asked if you and Tara arrived together this morning. 

**MEENAKSHI**
(Heartbeat faintly thumping in the audio)
Is that so? 

**HEADMASTER DAS**
She also asked if you had access to the chemistry lab's industrial bleach. Did you take any recently, Meenakshi?

## Episode 4: The Weak Link

[SFX: The ambient noise of the school hallway fades into tense silence.]

**MEENAKSHI**
(Smoothly)
Industrial bleach? No, Headmaster. The janitorial staff handles that. Why would Malini ask such a thing?

**HEADMASTER DAS**
She’s grasping at straws. The boy is a troublemaker. He probably ran off to Mumbai. Have a good class, Meenakshi.

[SFX: Footsteps walking away. Meenakshi ducks into a bathroom, turning on the tap to wash her face. The door squeaks open.]

**TARA**
(Whispering frantically)
Ma! Malini is pulling students out of class for questioning. She’s talking to Rohan’s friends. 

**MEENAKSHI**
Keep your voice down. What did they say?

**TARA**
They told her Rohan was coming to see me! They knew about the money, Ma. He bragged to them! 

[SFX: Heavy boots approaching the bathroom. The door pushes open. Malini enters.]

**MALINI**
Well. Both of you in here. Skipping first period, Tara?

**TARA**
(Stammering) 
I... I wasn't feeling well.

**MALINI**
I bet. Meenakshi, my officers searched the riverbank this morning. We found a smashed cell phone. 

**MEENAKSHI**
I hope it helps you find him.

**MALINI**
It might. We’re pulling the SIM card data now. But that’s not why I’m here. Tara, Rohan’s friends say he was blackmailing you over the stolen exam papers. Is that true?

**MEENAKSHI**
(Stepping in front of her daughter)
Inspector, you are border-lining on harassment. If you want to question my minor daughter, you will do it officially, with a lawyer present. 

**MALINI**
(Smirking coldly)
I don't need a lawyer, Meenakshi. I just need a search warrant for your backyard. And I think a judge will give me one.

## Episode 5: The Muddy Shoes

[SFX: Night crickets. Rain begins to lightly patter against the windows of House Number 4.]

**MEENAKSHI**
Pack a bag, Tara. Just clothes for three days.

**TARA**
Are we running? Ma, they'll know we did it if we run!

**MEENAKSHI**
We are not running. We are going to your aunt’s house in Mysore. By the time Malini gets her warrant tomorrow morning, we will be out of her jurisdiction, giving my lawyer time to block the search. 

[SFX: Zippers closing hastily. A loud thud from the backyard outside.]

**TARA**
(Gasping)
What was that?

[SFX: Meenakshi turns off the bedroom light. They creep to the window. Rain falls steadily outside.]

**MEENAKSHI**
(Whispering)
Someone is in the yard. By the mango tree. 

**TARA**
Is it the police? 

**MEENAKSHI**
No flashlights. They're sneaking. 

[SFX: A figure in a raincoat is seen digging near the compost pit with a small hand trowel.]

**TARA**
Ma... they're digging him up. We have to stop them!

**MEENAKSHI**
Wait. Look at the jacket. The reflective stripe. That’s not a police uniform. That’s a courier jacket. 

**TARA**
Rohan’s older brother... Dinesh. He works for the courier company. 

**MEENAKSHI**
He didn't trust Malini to do it fast enough. He came to find his brother himself. 

[SFX: A sharp scrape of metal hitting the plastic tarp beneath the mud. Dinesh stops digging. A horrifying silence. Then, Dinesh lets out a muffled, agonizing wail.]

**MEENAKSHI**
(Coldly)
He found him. Go to the kitchen, Tara. Get the largest knife we have. 

## Episode 6: The Cornered Animal

[SFX: The back door creaks open. The rain is heavier now. Meenakshi steps out into the mud, holding the large kitchen knife hidden behind her back.]

**MEENAKSHI**
Dinesh. 

[SFX: Dinesh scrambles backward in the mud, breathing heavily, pointing a trembling hand at the exposed blue tarp.]

**DINESH**
(Sobbing, enraged)
You killed him. You buried my baby brother like a dog!

**MEENAKSHI**
He fell, Dinesh. It was an accident. He was hurting Tara. 

**DINESH**
I’m calling Malini. I’m calling the whole precinct! 

[SFX: Dinesh fumbles for his phone. Meenakshi steps forward, the mud squelching beneath her feet.]

**MEENAKSHI**
If you call Malini, Tara’s life is over. She is a child. Your brother was a blackmailer and a thug. 

**DINESH**
Don't you take a step closer to me! 

**MEENAKSHI**
I have 50,000 rupees in my locker. It's yours. Take it, leave town, and never look back. 

**DINESH**
(Laughing hysterically)
Blood money? You think you can buy my brother's life?! 

[SFX: Dinesh dials three digits. The phone rings loudly in the rain.]

**DISPATCH**
(Through the phone)
Police emergency, what is your—

[SFX: A sickening thud as Meenakshi lunges, bringing the heavy, blunt handle of the knife down on the back of Dinesh's head. He collapses into the mud. The phone falls, still connected.]

**DISPATCH**
Hello? Is anyone there?

**MEENAKSHI**
(Breathing raggedly, she picks up the phone)
I'm sorry. Accidental dial. Everything is fine here. 

[SFX: Meenakshi hangs up. She looks down at Dinesh's unconscious body.]

**TARA**
(Standing in the doorway, horrified)
Ma... what did you just do?

**MEENAKSHI**
Get another tarp. 

## Episode 7: The Interrogation

[SFX: The harsh hum of fluorescent lights. A metal chair scrapes against a concrete floor. Police station interrogation room.]

**MALINI**
(Slamming a file on the table)
Stop playing with me, Meenakshi! Dinesh has been missing for 48 hours. The last GPS ping on his phone was a cell tower three blocks from your house. 

**MEENAKSHI**
(Calm, sipping tea)
I don't know what to tell you, Inspector. Perhaps he went looking for his brother. 

**MALINI**
My men are digging up your backyard right now. The judge signed the warrant. If Rohan or Dinesh are in that dirt, you are going to prison for the rest of your natural life. 

**MEENAKSHI**
Then I suggest we wait for your men to call. 

[SFX: Tense silence. A clock ticks loudly on the wall. Malini paces.]

**MALINI**
You think you're so smart. A quiet schoolteacher. But I see the ice in your eyes. You’d burn this whole town down to protect that girl. 

**MEENAKSHI**
A mother's job is to protect her child. Wouldn't you agree, Malini? Or did you forget how you covered up your own son's drunk driving accident three years ago?

[SFX: Malini stops pacing abruptly.]

**MALINI**
(Voice dropping to a dangerous whisper)
How do you know about that?

**MEENAKSHI**
I'm a teacher. I hear everything. I know you doctored the police report. If I go down, Inspector, I am dragging you and your son into the dirt with me. 

[SFX: The interrogation room door bursts open. A junior officer enters, out of breath.]

**OFFICER**
Inspector Malini! We finished digging up the backyard at Kaveri Lane. 

**MALINI**
And? What did you find?

**OFFICER**
Nothing, Ma'am. Just an old compost pit. The yard is completely clean. 

## Episode 8: Washed Clean

[SFX: A car driving down a deserted highway. The hum of the engine. Inside the car, it is quiet.]

**TARA**
(Voice shaking)
I can't believe they didn't find them. Where did you put them, Ma? 

**MEENAKSHI**
(Driving steadily)
Under the foundation of the new school gymnasium. They poured the concrete yesterday morning. Rohan and Dinesh are part of the architecture now. No one will ever look there. 

**TARA**
Malini looked like she wanted to kill you. 

**MEENAKSHI**
She knows. But she also knows I have her son's police report hidden in a safe deposit box. Mutually assured destruction, Tara. She will never come near us again. 

[SFX: The car pulls over to the side of a massive bridge. A roaring river rushes below.]

**MEENAKSHI**
Get out. Bring the trash bags from the trunk. 

[SFX: Car doors open. The wind howls across the bridge. They carry two heavy black garbage bags—filled with bloody clothes and the blue tarps—to the railing.]

**MEENAKSHI**
On three. Throw it as far into the current as you can. One. Two. Three!

[SFX: The bags sail through the air, hitting the raging water below and immediately vanishing into the dark.]

**TARA**
(Crying softly, finally relieved)
It's over. It's really over. 

**MEENAKSHI**
Yes. Look at me, Tara. You are going to graduate. You are going to go to a good college. You will have a beautiful life. 

**TARA**
I will, Ma. I promise. 

**MEENAKSHI**
Good. Now get in the car. We have grading to do before Monday. 

[SFX: Car doors shut. The engine revs, and the car drives off into the night, leaving only the sound of the roaring river.]
