# THE EMBER CROWN

## Episode 1: The Flame in the Lens

[SFX: The busy hum of Bengaluru traffic. Inside a quiet darkroom, the rhythmic clicking of a camera shutter.]

**KABIR**
(Muttering)
Hold still... catching the light off the brass... perfect. 

[SFX: A heavy thud upstairs. Footsteps descending a wooden staircase, accompanied by a sizzling sound—like water on a hot pan.]

**KABIR**
Mr. Rao? The studio is closed!

**CULTIST**
(Voice raspy, unnaturally distorted)
The Agni-Vamsha demands the final vessel. Hand over the heirloom, photographer. 

**KABIR**
What? Heirloom? I just shoot weddings! Look, I'm calling the police.

[SFX: Kabir fumbles with his phone. A sudden WHOOSH of fire ignites in the enclosed space.]

**KABIR**
(Shouting)
What the hell?! Your hands are on fire! 

**CULTIST**
The fire is our birthright. And the relic around your neck belongs to the deity trapped beneath the ash!

[SFX: The Cultist lunges. A glass tray shatters. Kabir is thrown against the counter.]

**KABIR**
Get off me! 

[SFX: A window shatters. A swift physical struggle. A woman grunts, landing a heavy kick. The Cultist crashes into a shelf.]

**NAINA**
(Breathless, commanding)
Get up! Now!

**KABIR**
Who are you?!

**NAINA**
My name is Naina. I’ve been tracking these fanatics for six months. They want the pendant your grandfather left you. It’s the final lock on the prison of a three-thousand-year-old fire deity. You are the last living heir. Take my hand and run!

[SFX: An explosion of fire blows the darkroom door off its hinges.]

## Episode 2: The Escape

[SFX: A roaring motorcycle engine tearing through Bengaluru nighttime traffic. Horns blaring.]

**KABIR**
(Yelling over the wind)
Are you crazy?! You just drove us through a red light! 

**NAINA**
(Shouting back)
They don't care about traffic laws, Kabir! Look behind us!

[SFX: Kabir shifts his weight on the bike. A roaring sound of fire trails behind them.]

**KABIR**
Is that... is that guy flying?! 

**NAINA**
They ride on currents of superheated air. Hold on! 

[SFX: Naina swerves the bike violently. A blast of fire scorches the asphalt right where they were just driving.]

**KABIR**
My grandfather was a watchmaker! He didn't guard ancient fire gods! 

**NAINA**
Your grandfather was a Sentinel of the Ash. He hid in plain sight. That camera charm around your neck? The silver is bound with celestial iron. If the Agni-Vamsha get it, they will resurrect the Ember Crown and turn this city into a furnace!

[SFX: The Cultist lands heavily on the roof of a parallel moving truck, raising his burning hands.]

**CULTIST**
(Roaring over the traffic)
The bloodline ends tonight! 

**NAINA**
Kabir! The pendant! Hold it up to him! Now! 

**KABIR**
Are you insane?! 

**NAINA**
Do it or we burn! 

[SFX: Kabir rips the pendant from his shirt and thrusts it forward. A blinding, high-frequency hum fills the air. The Cultist screams as the fire on his hands is suddenly violently sucked into the small silver charm. The Cultist collapses.]

**KABIR**
(Panting, terrified)
What... what did I just do?

**NAINA**
You just woke up. 

## Episode 3: The Safehouse

[SFX: A heavy metal door sliding shut and locking. A quiet, dusty underground bunker beneath an old fort.]

**KABIR**
(Pacing, hyperventilating)
Magic. Real, literal magic. Fireballs in the middle of Koramangala. I’m losing my mind. 

**NAINA**
(Wrapping a bandage around her arm)
Breathe, Kabir. Sit down before you pass out. 

**KABIR**
You want me to sit? A man threw fire at my face, Naina! How long have you known about this? Who are you, really? 

**NAINA**
I’m a scholar. I study the ancient elemental prisons. Three thousand years ago, the Ember Crown—a sentient force of pure destruction—was shattered into three relics. The cult found two. You have the third. 

[SFX: Kabir looks down at the pendant. It is glowing with a faint, pulsing orange light, like a dying ember.]

**KABIR**
It's warm. The metal is actually warm. 

**NAINA**
It fed on that cultist’s fire. It responds to your blood. But Kabir... the cult leader, a man named Agni, he won't send foot soldiers next time. He will come himself. 

**KABIR**
So we go to the police! The military! 

**NAINA**
Bullets melt before they reach Agni. The only way to stop him is to take the pendant to the original temple ruins in Hampi and re-seal the vault forever. 

**KABIR**
Hampi? That’s an eight-hour drive! We'll never make it! 

[SFX: A sudden, massive tremor shakes the bunker. Dust falls from the ceiling. The steel door begins to glow red-hot at the edges.]

**NAINA**
(Drawing a short, curved blade)
We don't have a choice. He's here. 

## Episode 4: The First Spark

[SFX: The steel door of the bunker groans and begins to warp inward, the metal glowing white-hot.]

**KABIR**
(Panicking)
There's no back exit, Naina! We're trapped! 

**NAINA**
Get behind me. When the door drops, I’ll blind him. You run for the stairs. 

**KABIR**
I'm not leaving you to die! 

[SFX: The steel door melts entirely, collapsing into a puddle of slag. Standing in the doorway is AGNI—a tall man wearing modern clothes, but his eyes are solid, burning embers.]

**AGNI**
(Voice echoing with power)
A scholar and a coward. A pathetic final stand for the great Sentinels. Give me the lock, boy, and I will let you turn to ash without pain. 

**NAINA**
Run, Kabir! 

[SFX: Naina charges, swinging her blade. Agni doesn't even move. He simply raises a finger. Naina is thrown backward violently by a shockwave of heat, crashing into the stone wall.]

**KABIR**
Naina! 

[SFX: Agni steps slowly into the room. The air sizzles around him.]

**AGNI**
She is broken. Now, the pendant. 

**KABIR**
(Voice shaking, backing away)
Stay back! 

[SFX: Kabir grips the pendant tightly. His heart beats rapidly. Suddenly, the pendant burns white-hot in his palm. Kabir screams, not in pain, but in shock as a sudden torrent of blue flame erupts from his own hand, blasting Agni in the chest.]

**AGNI**
(Grunting, stumbling backward in genuine surprise)
Blue fire? You... you don't just hold the lock. You hold the spark! 

[SFX: Kabir grabs Naina, throwing her arm over his shoulder, and uses the distraction to bolt past Agni into the smoking hallway.]

## Episode 5: The Toll of Ash

[SFX: The interior of a stolen jeep, speeding down a dirt highway at night. Rain beats against the windshield.]

**KABIR**
(Frantic)
Keep pressure on your ribs, Naina. We're almost to the state highway. 

**NAINA**
(Coughing weakly)
You... you channeled the blue flame, Kabir. Only the High Priests of the old world could do that. 

**KABIR**
I didn't do anything! I was terrified, I held the necklace, and my arm exploded. I can't do it again. I tried in the parking lot and nothing happened! 

**NAINA**
Because you were trying to force it. It responds to need. To protection. 

**KABIR**
Naina, you're bleeding internally. We have to stop at a hospital. 

**NAINA**
No! Agni's followers will find us. We have to get to the ruins at Hampi by sunrise, or the celestial alignment passes. If we miss it, the vault remains unlocked for another hundred years. 

**KABIR**
I don't care about the vault! I care that you are dying in my passenger seat! 

[SFX: Naina reaches over and grabs Kabir’s arm tightly.]

**NAINA**
Listen to me. My whole life... my family's whole legacy... was about making sure you survived to reach those ruins. If you stop the car, my life meant nothing. Do you understand? 

**KABIR**
(Tears in his voice)
I'm just a photographer. I didn't ask for any of this. 

**NAINA**
No one asks for their destiny, Kabir. They only decide what to do when it arrives. Keep driving. 

[SFX: Kabir shifts gears aggressively. The jeep roars faster into the stormy night.]

## Episode 6: The Temple of Cinders

[SFX: Wind howling through ancient, towering stone ruins. The crunch of boots on gravel. Dawn is breaking, casting long shadows.]

**KABIR**
(Supporting Naina)
We're here. The central courtyard. There's the altar. 

**NAINA**
(Breathing heavily, barely conscious)
The depression in the stone... place the pendant there. It will seal the vault. 

[SFX: A massive ring of fire suddenly ignites in a perfect circle around the courtyard, trapping them inside. The heat is intense.]

**AGNI**
(Stepping out from behind a stone pillar)
I must thank you, Kabir. I spent a decade trying to find the exact coordinates of this altar. You drove right to it. 

**KABIR**
Let her go, Agni. You want me. You want the relic. 

**AGNI**
I want the Ember Crown restored! For centuries, humanity has forgotten the purity of the flame. We hide behind electricity and engines. Today, the world burns and is born anew! 

[SFX: Agni raises both hands. The ring of fire shrinks, closing in on Kabir and Naina.]

**NAINA**
Kabir! Put it in the stone! Now! 

[SFX: Kabir lunges for the altar. Agni snaps his fingers. A whip of fire lashes out, striking Kabir's hand. He screams and drops the pendant. It clatters across the stone floor.]

**AGNI**
(Laughing)
Too slow, boy. 

[SFX: Agni walks toward the fallen pendant, picking it up. It glows fiercely in his grasp.]

**AGNI**
With this, the three pieces are reunited. The Crown is mine. 

**KABIR**
(Clutching his burned hand, looking at Naina on the ground)
No... 

## Episode 7: The Awakening

[SFX: The ground begins to shake violently. The ancient stones crack. A massive pillar of swirling fire erupts from the center of the altar into the sky.]

**AGNI**
(Screaming in triumph over the roar of the flames)
Yes! The Crown returns! 

[SFX: Agni steps into the pillar of fire. Instead of burning, he absorbs it. His voice becomes layered, deep, and demonic.]

**AGNI**
I AM THE INFERNO. 

**NAINA**
(Coughing blood)
Kabir... run. It's over. He has the power of a god. 

**KABIR**
(Staring at the flames, his voice eerily calm)
No. He doesn't. 

[SFX: Kabir stands up. He walks slowly toward the roaring pillar of fire. The intense ambient heat doesn't seem to affect him.]

**NAINA**
Kabir, what are you doing?! You'll burn! 

**KABIR**
The pendant was a lock, Naina. But a lock isn't the prison. The bloodline is the prison. My grandfather didn't pass down a piece of jewelry. He passed down the vault. It's inside me. 

[SFX: Kabir steps directly into the edge of the firestorm. Agni turns to him, enraged.]

**AGNI**
Mortal fool! You are nothing but kindling! 

[SFX: Agni unleashes a massive torrent of fire directly at Kabir. But instead of an explosion, there is a vacuum sound. The fire swirls around Kabir and is sucked into his chest.]

**AGNI**
(Stunned)
What?! Impossible! 

**KABIR**
(Eyes glowing bright blue)
This fire doesn't belong to you, Agni. It belongs to my family. And I am taking it back. 

## Episode 8: The Ember Crown

[SFX: The roar of the firestorm reaches a deafening pitch. Kabir stands firmly in the center, his entire body glowing with blue energy.]

**AGNI**
(Panicking, his voice losing its demonic edge)
Stop! You can't contain the Crown! It will tear your mortal body apart! 

**KABIR**
Watch me. 

[SFX: Kabir thrusts his hand forward. A massive vortex opens in the air between them. The fire surrounding Agni is violently ripped away, swirling into Kabir’s palm.]

**AGNI**
(Screaming as his power is drained)
No! A lifetime of devotion! It is mine! 

**KABIR**
Then burn with it! 

[SFX: A blinding flash and an ear-splitting boom. The shockwave shatters the ancient stone pillars around them. Then, absolute silence, save for the gentle crackle of a dying campfire.]

**KABIR**
(Breathing heavily, exhausted)
It's... it's done. 

[SFX: Footsteps as Kabir walks back to Naina. He kneels beside her.]

**NAINA**
(Weakly, smiling)
Your eyes... they're still glowing. 

**KABIR**
(Softly)
I feel it, Naina. All of it. The Crown isn't a thing to be worn. It's a responsibility. And it's safe now. Deep down. 

**NAINA**
Agni? 

**KABIR**
Just a man again. Unconscious. He'll answer to the mortal police now. 

**NAINA**
What about you, Kabir? You can't go back to shooting weddings with a deity inside you. 

**KABIR**
(Helping her up)
No. I suppose I can't. You said there were other elemental prisons, right? 

**NAINA**
(Laughing, wincing in pain)
Scores of them. 

**KABIR**
Well. We better get you patched up, then. We have a lot of work to do. 

[SFX: The crunch of gravel as the two walk away into the dawn light. A final, faint hum of blue fire, and a camera shutter clicks one last time.]
